# Galvyx Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: TBD

Galvyx is a local-first field-notes, photos, expenses, and report-generation app for site visits.

## Information you enter into the app

Galvyx may store information that you type or capture inside the app, including:

- Client or site names
- Project names
- Technician names
- Site visit notes
- Device information
- Expense entries
- Photos and captions
- Generated PDF reports

## Where your data is stored

Galvyx stores site visit information locally on your device. Galvyx does not currently provide a cloud account, backend sync service, analytics service, advertising service, or developer-operated database.

Photos captured through Galvyx are stored in app-owned storage on your device. PDF reports are generated locally on your device.

## Sharing reports

Galvyx only shares information when you choose to export or share a report using Android's share sheet. The destination app or service you choose, such as email, messaging, cloud storage, or ticketing software, may process the report under its own privacy practices.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual notes, devices, expenses, photos, and site visit records inside Galvyx. Deleting a photo captured by Galvyx removes the app-owned photo file when possible.

Uninstalling Galvyx may remove app-owned local data according to Android system behavior and backup settings.

## Permissions

Galvyx may use camera/photo and file-sharing capabilities so you can capture visit photos and share generated PDF reports. Galvyx does not use these permissions for advertising or background tracking.

## Children's privacy

Galvyx is intended for business and field-service use. It is not directed to children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as Galvyx gains new features, especially if cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or third-party integrations are added.
