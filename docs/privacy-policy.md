# Galvyx Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-21

Galvyx is an Android field-reporting app for capturing site visit notes, photos, device details, expenses, and PDF reports.

## Information stored by the app

Galvyx stores the information you enter or capture in the app, which may include:

- Client or site names
- Project names
- Technician names
- Site visit notes
- Device information
- Expense entries
- Photos and captions
- Generated PDF reports
- Company/report profile settings

## Local storage

Galvyx stores site visit information on your device. Galvyx does not require an account and does not operate a cloud sync service, analytics service, advertising service, or developer-operated database.

Photos captured through Galvyx are stored in app-owned storage on your device. PDF reports are generated locally on your device.

## Sharing reports

Galvyx shares information only when you choose to export or share a report using Android's share sheet. The destination app or service you select, such as email, messaging, cloud storage, or ticketing software, may process the report under its own privacy practices.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual notes, devices, expenses, photos, and site visit records inside Galvyx. When you delete a photo captured by Galvyx, the app removes the app-owned photo file when Android storage permissions allow it.

Uninstalling Galvyx removes app-owned local data according to Android system behavior and backup settings.

## Permissions

Galvyx uses camera and file-sharing capabilities so you can capture visit photos and share generated PDF reports. Galvyx does not use these permissions for advertising, background tracking, or analytics.

## Children's privacy

Galvyx is intended for business and field-service use. It is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Galvyx adds features that change how data is stored, processed, or shared.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on Galvyx's Google Play listing.
