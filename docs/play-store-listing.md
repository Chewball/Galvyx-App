# Galvyx Play Store Listing

## App name

Galvyx

## Short description

Field notes, photos, expenses, and PDF reports for site visits.

## Full description

Galvyx helps field technicians document site visits from an Android phone. Capture the details that matter while you are onsite, then export a clean PDF report when the work is ready to share.

With Galvyx, you can:

- Create site visit records with client, project, technician, date, and job type.
- Add categorized work notes.
- Capture and caption site photos.
- Record device details such as hostname, IP address, serial number, MAC address, manufacturer, model, and location.
- Track expenses by date, category, vendor, amount, payment method, and notes.
- Search and filter recent visits.
- Edit visit details and captured records before sending a report.
- Export a PDF report with grouped notes, devices, expenses, photos, totals, timestamps, and page numbers.

Galvyx is built for local-first field documentation. It does not require an account, does not include ads, does not include analytics tracking, and does not upload your visit records to a Galvyx server. Your records stay on your device unless you choose to share an exported report through Android's share sheet.

## Data Safety notes for Google Play

These notes match the current app behavior:

- Account creation: No.
- Ads: No.
- Analytics: No.
- Data collected by developer: No developer-side collection by default.
- Data shared by developer: No developer-side sharing by default.
- User-entered content: Stored locally on device.
- Photos: Stored locally in app-owned storage.
- Reports: Generated locally and shared only when the user chooses a share destination.
- Data deletion: Supported in the app for visits and captured records.

## Content rating notes

Galvyx is intended for business productivity and field-service documentation. The app does not include social features, gambling, user-generated public feeds, or mature content features.
